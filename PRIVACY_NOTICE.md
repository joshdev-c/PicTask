# PicTask privacy notice — draft

**Replace the bracketed operator details and have this notice reviewed before a real-user pilot.** This is a product draft, not legal advice.

## What we collect

- Your email address and account identifier to create and secure your account.
- The document details you review and save, such as provider, amount, due date, and optional account/reference information.
- Tasks, reminder settings, and completion status.
- An original document image only when you expressly choose **Save image and reviewed details**.

## Why we use it

We use this information only to create your private task plans, calendar reminders, and Vault records. PicTask does not sell personal data, use advertising trackers, send scans to cloud AI, or make payments on your behalf.

## Storage and sharing

Your signed-in records are stored in [SUPABASE PROJECT REGION/PROVIDER] and limited to your account through access-control policies. Scans are recognized on your device. A source image is not uploaded when you select **Details only** or **Discard image after task creation**.

## Retention and control

Records remain until you delete them or delete your account. You can export data, correct saved details, restore an archived record, request password reset, and permanently delete your account and associated app records from Profile.

## Your choices and permissions

Camera and photo access are requested only when you choose to scan or import a document. Notification permission is requested only when a due-date reminder is scheduled. Biometric/device-credential access is optional and protects Vault access on your device.

## Contact and rights requests

For access, correction, deletion, portability, or privacy questions, contact **[OPERATOR NAME / PRIVACY CONTACT / EMAIL]**. For Philippine users, the operator will handle requests in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable National Privacy Commission guidance.

## Changes

We will notify users in the app before material changes to this notice take effect.
